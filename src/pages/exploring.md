## Video Games

My pandemic lockdown discovery was video games. I played occasionally as a kid on my
Nintendo64 (!) but in the last few years, I’ve really come to appreciate the medium and find joy in learning how to play new games. It’s genuinely been a helpful way for me to get comfortable trying something outside of my comfort zone, to fail without risk, and to challenge myself to get better at something that doesn’t come to me easily.

Solo, I’ve played Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, Stray, Portal, and Portal 2. I’m currently playing The Talos Principle. As co-op, I’ve played Split Fiction, Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime, and It Takes Two with my husband, Caleb. I couldn’t handle the pressure of Overcooked.

With friends, I play MarioKart, Mario Party, and Super Smash Bros. Caleb and I also do an
annual MarioKart Tournament where we drive all 48 courses in one day and the ultimate winner is champion for a year. It is not relevant who is the current champion.
